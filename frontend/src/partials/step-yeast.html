How the attenuation target works

The published attenuation is the strain's apparent ceiling. The cap is the high end minus the batch derate. The solver clamps the malt's fermentability to that cap; it does not force attenuation up to it.

Mash temperature is guidance, not a hidden input. 148–150°F suggests 0–1 derate points; 151–153°F suggests 1–3; 154–156°F suggests 3–5; 157°F+ suggests 5 or more. Enter the derate you intend, or use Set attenuation for a ranged strain.

STA1+ consumes malt dextrin beyond the ordinary cap, and sugar extract is never capped, so realized attenuation can rise above the selected cap — and above 100 % apparent. The above-cap row names the cause when that happens.

POF is the phenolic off-flavour phenotype and is unrelated to attenuation. It is shown only as a strain fact.
