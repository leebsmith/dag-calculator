Pin Target Ions

Checked ions are held as equality targets; unchecked ions float to the source.

Pin one to four. The rest float, and the solver keeps a degree of freedom for the others. More than four overdetermines the salts.

Up to four is the rule, not a promise: salts only add, so pinning an ion the source already meets forbids every salt that adds it — the solve will say so rather than reach the target.
