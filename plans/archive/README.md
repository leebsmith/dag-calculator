# Archived Plans

Completed, executed, or superseded plans, grouped by completion month. Nothing
here is current work; see `TODO.md` and the top-level `plans/` directory for
active or proposed plans. A superseded plan states its successor in the
frontmatter and at the top of its body.

New plans are archived by `/goal` into the current `YYYY-MM/` folder. The
top-level archive holds only this index; `tests/guards/archive-layout.test.js`
enforces the layout.
