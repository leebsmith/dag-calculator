# The Belgian knob — retired

**Status:** retired. The advisory was a special-case calculation: hold apparent
extract, raise original extract with sucrose to a target apparent attenuation or
ABV. It assumed sucrose, `F = 1`, a single dose, and the old fixed-AA ray.

It is replaced by the three scale pins (`ABV`, `OG`, `FG`) plus the authored
sugar composition (`dose` or `share`). The general inverse — fix ABV and FG,
solve the composition — is deferred as the ABV+FG composition-solving mode and
is **not** one of the three recipe pins.

The fermentation-space graph remains the spatial view; it is pin-aware and shows
the ray only when ABV is the pin. Real scales and a refreshed palette are a
later pass.
