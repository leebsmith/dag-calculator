# Hop documents

Prose for hop bitterness, product forms, and timing. `data/artifact.json` owns
the hop data and the solvers own the maths; these documents explain and annotate.

| Document | Status | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`whirlpool-hopping-model.md`](whirlpool-hopping-model.md) | living — the contract | whirlpool bitterness theory, the cooling curve, effective boiling-equivalent time, Tinseth utilisation, product-form multipliers, and the reference vectors |
| [`reference/hot-side-hopping-agent-dosing.md`](reference/hot-side-hopping-agent-dosing.md) | background | generated conversation on hot-side product forms, dosing, and oil retention; supplies the bands behind the product-form multipliers |
| [`reference/dry-hopping-times-and-conditions.md`](reference/dry-hopping-times-and-conditions.md) | background | generated conversation on when dry hops are added and what ends their contact; source for the dry-hop card vocabularies |

Background documents are imported verbatim and are not maintained here. Where a
background document disagrees with the model document or with
`data/artifact.json`, the artifact wins.
