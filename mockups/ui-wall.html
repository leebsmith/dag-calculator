Discussion

Adapted from the 2019 homebrew club pale ale, scaled to the HERMS rig. Crystal 40 replaces the original Crystal 60 for a drier finish.

Hop strategy

IBU split between the boil and the hot whirlpool; flavour and aroma additions use Cryo in the cool whirlpool.

  • Sensory: 60/40 Chinook/Simcoe on the cool side by weight.
  • Pine backbone with a grapefruit and citrus surround.

Fermentation strategy

Pitch at 18 °C, hold 19 °C through high krausen, then free-rise to 21 °C for a clean finish. Dry hop at high krausen.

Process notes

0.5 oz (14 g) lemon peel at flame-out. 0.05 oz (1.5 g) fresh-grated ginger at flame-out.
